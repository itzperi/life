# 90-day plan (16 Nov 2026 to 14 Feb 2027)

## Honest expectations
- **Organic search** for non-brand queries ("manpower supplier chennai") typically takes **6 to 9 months** to reach page 1, competing against JustDial, IndiaMART, Randstad and TeamLease. Nothing here guarantees a ranking.
- **What works from week 1:** the Business Profile (it already has 4.6★ and 178 reviews), WhatsApp response speed, directory listings (JustDial and IndiaMART rank for almost every query), and paid search plus Meta click-to-WhatsApp.
- **100-200 qualified employer enquiries a day** is a stretch goal. The realistic path is paid search and Meta for volume in months 1-3, with organic and AI citations taking a growing share from month 4. `08-paid-bridge.md` has the funnel maths with assumptions. Expect real numbers to replace those assumptions after week 3.
