## D. Hard rules
1. Never invent clients, testimonials, ratings, awards, statistics, headcounts, turnaround times, fees, licences, branches or years.
2. You may name well-known public employers or industrial estates in a city **only as local context** (for example "Hyundai's plant at Irungattukottai"). Never imply that they are Lifeline clients. City pages carry a note saying so. Include only facts you are highly confident about, or that you verified with a search. If unsure, leave the fact out.
3. **Security guards:** supplying private security guards in India needs a PSARA licence. Do **not** offer security guard supply anywhere until the owner confirms the licence. Housekeeping, facility support and front-office staff are fine.
4. **Overseas placement:** do not mention it. It needs an MEA recruiting-agent licence.
5. **Candidates:** never say or imply that candidates pay a fee. Job-seeker content goes on /jobs/ only.
6. Write for employers: plant HR managers, facility managers, clinic owners, operations heads. Use plain Indian English and short sentences. No hype words: "best", "leading", "No. 1", "world-class", "100%".
7. Under every question heading, start with a direct answer of 40 to 60 words, then add detail.
8. Each industry or city page must say something specific that no other page says. Do not write near-duplicate pages from a template.
9. Every page ends with a next step: Call or WhatsApp +91 99622 99076.
10. Statutory figures (EPF, ESI, wages, labour codes) must carry an "as of" date and a source.

## E. Regulatory context (verify before quoting; as of Nov 2026)
- The four Labour Codes took effect on 21 Nov 2025. Under the OSH Code, the contract labour licence applies to contractors with 50 or more contract workers (up from 20 under the old CLRA Act). State rules vary. Sources: treelife.in, india-briefing.com.
- ESI: employer 3.25%, employee 0.75%, for wages up to Rs 21,000 a month.
- EPF: 12% employer + 12% employee. Several sources report the wage ceiling rose from Rs 15,000 to Rs 25,000 on 17 Sep 2026 (S.O. 5109(E)). Confirm with EPFO.
- Tamil Nadu minimum wages were revised from 1 Apr 2026 (notification dated 18 Feb 2026). The reported shops-and-establishments VDA is Rs 7,353 a month. Confirm with the TN Labour Department.
- Market fee ranges published by vendors (not Lifeline's prices): permanent placement 8.33% to 16.67% of annual CTC; contract staffing markup quoted from 5% up to 15% or more, plus 18% GST (for manpower supply, generally charged on the full invoice; confirm with a GST adviser). Replacement windows of 30 to 90 days are common.
