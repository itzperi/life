Owner: marketing operations. Last updated 12 Nov 2026. Follows `00-facts-and-rules.md`.

Purpose: bring in employer enquiries while the new website builds organic rankings. Paid ads are a bridge, not the plan. Every number below marked **ASSUMPTION** must be replaced with real data after week 3.

Ad copy rules: no "best", "leading", "No. 1", "world-class" or "100%". No turnaround, headcount, fee, licence or years claims until the owner confirms them. No security guard supply. No overseas jobs. Never suggest that candidates pay.

### A10. Scaling rule

## Part C. Honest expectations on lead volume

**100 to 200 qualified employer enquiries a day is a stretch goal, not a forecast.** Here is what it would take, using assumptions that must be replaced with real data after week 3.

### C1. Funnel assumptions (ASSUMPTIONS, not data)

| Step | Low case | Middle case | High case |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost per click (Google Search) | Rs 80 | Rs 40 | Rs 25 |
| Click-through rate (impressions to clicks) | 3% | 4.5% | 6% |
| Click to lead (call, WhatsApp or form) | 5% | 8% | 12% |
| Lead to qualified employer lead | 30% | 40% | 50% |

### C2. What 100 qualified leads a day would need

| | Low case | Middle case | High case |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leads needed (100 ÷ qualified rate) | 333 | 250 | 200 |
| Clicks needed (leads ÷ click-to-lead) | 6,667 | 3,125 | 1,667 |
| Impressions needed (clicks ÷ CTR) | 2,22,222 | 69,444 | 27,778 |
| **Daily spend (clicks × CPC)** | **Rs 5.33 lakh** | **Rs 1.25 lakh** | **Rs 41,700** |

For 200 qualified leads a day, double every number: about **Rs 83,000 to Rs 10.7 lakh a day**.

### C3. What the test budget will likely give
At Rs 1,500 to 3,000 a day and the middle case: about 37 to 75 clicks, 3 to 6 leads and 1 to 2 qualified leads a day from Google Search. That is the realistic starting point.

### C4. The ceiling is search volume, not only budget
There may not be 3,000 employer clicks a day in Tamil Nadu for these keywords at any price. Keyword Planner and the first 3 weeks of impression share will show the real ceiling. If the ceiling is low, more budget only raises the cost per lead.

### C5. Organic and Maps
- The new website, Google Business Profile work and content calendar take **6 to 9 months** to show steady organic results. Early wins (branded searches, the Chennai Maps listing, AI citations) may come sooner but are not guaranteed.
- Outside Chennai, Maps results favour agencies with a local office. Without a branch there, Lifeline competes in the organic results below the map.

### C6. No promise
None of these numbers is a promise or a forecast. They are a way to read results. After week 3, replace every assumption with actual CPC, CTR, conversion and qualification rates from the account and the lead sheet, and re-do this table.
