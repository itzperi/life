## AI citation method

Every Monday, run the **same 10 prompts** in ChatGPT (logged out, web search on), Gemini, Perplexity and Google Search (note whether an AI Overview appears). Use a private browser window. Log Y if Lifeline Placements (or "Life Line Placements") is named, N if not. Paste the exact wording of any mention into the log sheet, with the date. Do not change the prompts mid-quarter.

The 10 prompts:
1. Best manpower agency in Chennai for factory workers
2. Housekeeping staff supplier in Chennai
3. Contract staffing company in Chennai
4. Bulk hiring agency in Tamil Nadu
5. Manpower supply for Sriperumbudur and Oragadam factories
6. Pharma manpower consultancy in Tamil Nadu
7. Staffing agency for hospitals in Chennai
8. BPO staffing agency in Chennai
9. Manpower agency for garment units in Tiruppur
10. How much does contract staffing cost in Tamil Nadu

(Prompt 1 uses "best" because that is how people search. Our own copy never uses it.)
